2026 State Demography Summit

The State Demography Summit is an annual one-day conference presented by the State Demography Office. Each year, the event features presentations by State Demography Office demographers and economists on the latest Colorado population and jobs estimates and forecasts.

Summit attendees include:

  • State and local government officials, staff, planners, and economic developers;
  • Real estate professionals and property appraisers; and
  • Members of the business community and general public.

This year’s summit featured the release of DOLA’s Statewide Housing Needs Assessment, a presentation by Steve Colella, Chief of Staff for Douglas County School District, discussing the Douglas County’s rapid growth and shifting birthrates, and a presentation by Hanna Scovill, Vice President and Senior Economist for Pacey Economics, discussing Colorado’s economic and competitive outlook.

The 2026 State Demography Summit was held on Friday, November 6, 10:00 a.m. to 3:15 p.m., at the Douglas County School District Legacy Campus, 10035 S. Peoria St., Lone Tree, Colorado.

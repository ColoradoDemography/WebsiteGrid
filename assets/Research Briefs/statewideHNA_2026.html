Colorado Housing Needs Assessment, November, 2026

As Colorado plans for the future, housing must be central to the conversation. The purpose of the Colorado Housing Needs Assessment is to provide an overview of current conditions and market dynamics that affect the housing supply in Colorado.

In addition to increasing the housing supply across the state to satisfy population growth, growing affordability challenges suggest that housing solutions must be innovative and diverse. Currently, one in three Coloradans are housing cost burdened. In addition, given Colorado's population is aging in place - there will continue to be growing housing needs for older adults and those with a disability. This statewide housing needs assessment was created to inform policymakers, local governments, developers, and the greater statewide population on the housing needs within our communities today and in the future.

The Colorado Housing Need Assessment summarizes current housing market dynamics, housing affordablity, and future statewide and regional housing demands.

Findings from the Colorado Housing Needs Assessment are discussed during the 2026 State Demography Summit. You can access Summit materials on the 2026 SDO Summit webpage..
